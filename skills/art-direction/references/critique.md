# Critique

Passing `inspect.mjs` means nothing banned was measured. It does not mean the page is good. Open the screenshots (`<out>/<width>-first-screen.jpg` and `<width>-full.jpg`) and go through these passes, at all three widths.

## Passes
1. **First look.** Cover everything but the first screen. In three seconds: what is this, who is it for, what do I do? If you cannot say, the headline or the hero failed.
2. **Focal point.** Does each screen have one thing the eye lands on first? Two equal things compete; none is a void.
3. **The idea.** Is the idea sentence visible without being told? Would the nearest competitor's page look like this with the logo swapped? If yes, the idea stayed in the notes.
4. **Rhythm.** Do neighbouring sections change layout, scale and ground? Three similar bands in a row read as a template.
5. **Type.** Is there one big thing and several small things, with few sizes between? Are the line lengths under 75 characters? Does the headline use the typeface's character, or only fill space?
6. **Color.** Is the accent rare? Do fields and ground have real contrast between them? Does any color come from the subject?
7. **Pictures.** Are they large, relevant and varied in crop? Does a stock-looking image stand in for the subject? Is a credit missing?
8. **Fullness.** Does every desktop screen carry content, not just ground? Is there at most one deliberate rest?
9. **Density of proof.** Is each claim next to its evidence? Is any proof invented?
10. **Live thing.** Does it work with a thumb? Does it say something specific?
11. **Phone.** Is the phone layout its own composition or a squeezed desktop? Is anything cut, covered or sideways?
12. **Words.** Any stock phrase, any caption that explains what the picture is not, any call to action that names the system?
13. **Last screen.** Does the page end on a decision (the one action) and a real footer, or does it trail off?
14. **Restraint.** Is there one memorable element and everything else quiet? Remove one accessory.

## Score (for your own tracking, 1 to 5 each)
Idea · Composition · Type · Color · Pictures · Fullness · Phone · Craft (focus, semantics, alt text, performance). A 5 means a designer would not change it; most first builds are 2 or 3. The weakest axis sets the order of fixes.

## Round
1. Write the critique down: for each pass that failed, one line saying what and where.
2. Fix the top three by impact, not the easiest three.
3. Run `inspect.mjs` again and look again.
4. Stop after three rounds. Report what is still weak instead of polishing forever.

Do not hide things from the meter to turn a FAIL green (a pixel of text to dodge the blank-stretch check, an invisible picture to reach the floor). The meter exists to catch what the eye skips.
